How I work with AI

My starting point is always the operational need.

AI is part of how I work every day. I use it to sharpen ideas, surface problems early, and test solutions quickly, always in service of what the business and its people need.

Find the friction

The handoff that drops, the request that sits in someone's inbox, the spreadsheet nobody trusts. Fixing the process comes before automating it.

Automate with today's tools

I use Claude, Claude Cowork, and ChatGPT to build and run workflows across Gmail, Google Drive, Slack, and other software, using connectors and MCP to bring tools and context together. I match the platform to the task, shape the instructions and context, and refine the workflow through hands-on iteration.

Keep judgment human

AI can draft, sort, and summarize. Decisions about people, money, and trust stay with people, and I design workflows so that line is always clear.
